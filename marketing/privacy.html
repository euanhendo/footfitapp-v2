FootFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

The short version

FootFit does not collect your data. Everything the app knows about you stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

FootFit stores your foot measurements (length and width in millimetres), your sock and sport preferences, and your list of owned shoes. All of this is saved only in your device's secure storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — FootFit has no server, no account system, and no analytics.

The camera

The foot scanner uses your camera to measure your foot against a reference object (a sheet of A4 paper). Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere. FootFit does not request microphone access.

Third-party links

Boot and sock listings link to retailers (for example, Pro:Direct) so you can buy a recommended fit. Tapping a link opens the retailer's site, which has its own privacy policy. Product images in the app are loaded from retailer websites; those requests carry no personal data beyond what any image download involves (such as your IP address).

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes

If a future version of FootFit changes any of this — for example, by adding optional accounts — this policy will be updated first, and the app will ask before anything leaves your device.
